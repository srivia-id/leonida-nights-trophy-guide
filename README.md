LEONIDA NIGHTS is an offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. Every trophy is shown with its official name, description and grade as PlayStation publishes them, plus a guide written in our own words — every step cites its source, so a reader can see where each fact comes from. The guide adds a platinum roadmap in seven stages (S1–S7), a missables watch list (one possibly missable trophy, five more to watch, with the sources' disagreement on the meditation count shown in the open), a spoiler setting that hides story trophies until they are earned, and a checklist whose progress is saved in the browser and can be exported to a file and imported back. Filters cover story, missable, collectibles, activities, side, crime, money and misc trophies, and counters track the numbers the game itself counts (18 meditations, 42 shooting-range golds, 250 stunts, 100 vehicles, and more). No Rockstar artwork: the look is our own, and the app loads nothing from the network.
